Screen Calibration

We need two real-world numbers to size letters correctly: how big a pixel actually is on your screen, and how far your eye is from it. Without this, any "6/6" result is a guess.

Step 1 — Hold a standard credit/debit card flat against your screen.

Drag until the bar below exactly matches the card's width.

300 px
Step 2 — Viewing Distance

Tip: measure with a tape, or sit at a comfortable arm's length (~40cm).

Before You Start

For the most reliable estimate, please ensure:

• Lighting is comfortable & even.
• Blink often to avoid dryness.
• Screen brightness is at a normal indoor level.
• You stay at the distance you calibrated for.
• Take breaks if your eyes feel strained.
